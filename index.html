Weather-first model

A regularized nonlinear model uses 15 observed weather/ocean fields, AM/PM, and three lagged encounter-state measures. It contains no date, month, day-of-year, or seasonal predictor. For every validation day in 2025–2026, the model is freshly trained on all earlier trips; that day remains unseen.

Range calibration: signed actual-minus-forecast residuals create asymmetric 50% and 80% ranges. Each validation range uses only earlier errors; its 80% band captured of later outcomes.

Interpretation: this is genuine rolling-origin weather validation, but accuracy remains modest. Forecast ranges matter more than exact point estimates.
